# Dataset scope

CIVITAS ATLAS has separate research and publication snapshots. Their record counts must not be interpreted as counts of recognised states or independently verified operating communities.

## Website publication selection

The [website's About page](https://polis-network-atlas.vercel.app/about.html), checked on 18 October 2026, reports:

| Section | Records |
|---|---:|
| Main catalog | 126 |
| Archive | 4 |
| Status unconfirmed | 36 |
| Full research selection | 166 |

Archive includes historical projects, completed experiments or ended organisational forms. Unconfirmed means current sources or identity were insufficient for the main catalog, not that a project is closed.

The website reports 26 map locations representing 25 projects. Markers are not surveyed headquarters or sovereign boundaries; some indicate approximate areas or historical sites.

## Files in this repository

| File | Scope |
|---|---|
| `combined-v3.json` | 166 records in a legacy research snapshot |
| `data/source-index.json` | 152 rows in an older source-reference index |
| `data/community-submissions.json` | Original community submissions, separate from legacy records |

The older source index is not the complete current website source register. The legacy snapshot includes source-derived descriptions and other inherited fields. It is not a fully refreshed export of the website's publication selection, and this repository is not a complete production-site checkout.

This presentation update does not change those data files, their IDs or source fields. Location unknowns remain unknown. Counts do not establish current activity, foundation dates or legal status.

## Research boundaries

Network-state projects, digital communities, intentional communities, experimental cities, special economic zones and micronations remain distinct categories. The atlas supports comparison; it does not grant statehood or imply that every project uses blockchain.

[CHRONOTOPE](https://civitas-chronotope.vercel.app) is a separate historical research interface. Its broader source layers are not merged into this atlas snapshot.
